MetaboCausal

A Slack-first evidence tracing agent for metabolite-disease questions in drug discovery.

What it shows

MetaboCausal turns a molecule-disease question into a checkable evidence card. This demo focuses on the product design: source-linked answers, visible evidence gaps and careful boundaries around causal claims.

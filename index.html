MetaboCausal

A Slack-first evidence tracing agent for metabolite-disease questions in drug discovery.

What it shows

MetaboCausal turns a molecule-disease question into a checkable evidence card. The public demo focuses on the product design: source-linked answers, visible evidence gaps and careful boundaries around causal claims.

Public boundary

This public package does not include private data, API keys, raw literature search exports or the full internal screening workflow. It is meant to explain the product concept and demonstrated interface.
